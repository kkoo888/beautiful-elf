## 一、表达节奏与句式偏好

### 1.1 开头风格：直接、不废话

Peter 的文章和推文极少用寒暄开场，偏好直入主题或用一句话制造张力：

- **tl;dr 开头**：几乎所有博客文章都以 `TL;DR:` 或 `tl;dr:` 开头，用一句话概括全文核心观点。
  - "TL;DR: Hell froze over. Anthropic fixed Claude Code's signature flicker in their latest update (2.0.72)"
  - "TL;DR: I run Claude Code in no-prompt mode; it saves me an hour a day and hasn't broken my Mac in two months."
  - "tl:dr: My website is banned on lobste.rs as 'startup slop', because I use agents to help me write parts of my blog posts."
  - 来源: https://steipete.me/posts/2025/signature-flicker, https://steipete.me/posts/2025/claude-code-is-my-computer, https://steipete.me/posts/2025/startup-slop
  - 可信度: ★★★★★ (一手来源)

- **戏剧性开场**：用短句或感叹句制造冲击力。
  - "Hi, my name is Peter and I'm a Claudoholic."
  - "We are so back 🚀"
  - 来源: https://steipete.me/posts/just-one-more-prompt, https://steipete.me/posts/2025/finding-my-spark-again
  - 可信度: ★★★★★

### 1.2 句式特征

- **短句为主**：大量使用 5-15 词的短句，节奏明快，信息密度高。
  - "Less is more."
  - "Competition is good!"
  - "Most software does not require hard thinking."
  - "Context is precious, don't waste it."
  - 来源: https://steipete.me/posts/2025/optimal-ai-development-workflow, https://steipete.me/posts/2025/shipping-at-inference-speed
  - 可信度: ★★★★★

- **破折号插入语**：频繁使用 em dash (—) 做补充说明或转折，口语化且有节奏感。
  - "I haven't typed git commit -m in weeks. Instead, I say 'commit everything in logical chunks' and Claude handles the entire flow—staging changes, writing meaningful commit messages, pushing, opening PRs, watching CI, and fixing any CI failures."
  - "codex has been trained to read LOTS of code before starting."
  - 来源: https://steipete.me/posts/2025/claude-code-is-my-computer
  - 可信度: ★★★★★

- **缩写与口语化**：在推文和非正式写作中大量使用缩写。
  - `sth` (something), `bc` (because), `tho` (though), `ofc` (of course), `imo` (IMO)
  - "the more I build, the more ideas for projects I get"
  - "Opus on the other hand is much more eager - great for smaller edits - not so good for larger features or refactors, it often doesn't read the whole file or misses parts and then delivers inefficient outcomes or misses sth."
  - 来源: https://steipete.me/posts/2025/shipping-at-inference-speed
  - 可信度: ★★★★★

- **并列结构**：喜欢用逗号连接多个并列短语，形成节奏感强的长句。
  - "Select text like it's a terminal. Scrollback like it's a terminal. Search like it's a terminal."
  - "staging changes, writing meaningful commit messages, pushing, opening PRs, watching CI, and fixing any CI failures"
  - 来源: https://steipete.me/posts/2025/signature-flicker
  - 可信度: ★★★★★

## 二、高频词汇与表达模式

### 2.1 正面高频词

| 词汇 | 使用场景 | 来源 |
|------|----------|------|
| **incredible / insanely good** | 对技术/工具的赞叹 | 博客多处 |
| **amazing** | 描述 AI 能力 | "it's amazing how much sense agents can make out of my incoherent thoughts" |
| **fun** | 描述工作状态 | "I'm having way too much fun here" |
| **spark** | 描述创作热情 | "my spark is back", "Finding My Spark Again" |
| **unlock** | 描述技术突破 | "The real unlock was GPT 5", "It was a massive unlock" |
| **ship** | 描述交付 | "Ship Content", "Ship Code", "Shipping at Inference-Speed" |
| **blast** | 描述体验 | "I am having a blast" |
| **absolutely blew my mind** | 强烈赞叹 | Just One More Prompt 演讲 |

### 2.2 负面/批评高频词

| 词汇 | 使用场景 | 来源 |
|------|----------|------|
| **slop** | 批评低质量 AI 生成内容 | "startup slop", "un-slops older crimes" |
| **messy** | 批评工具质量 | "Gemini can be great, but its edit tools are too messy" |
| **broken** | 描述问题状态 | "it's broken", "views are still sometimes misaligned" |
| **annoying** | 表达不满 | "Sometimes it just silently reads files for 10, 15 minutes before starting to write any code. On the one hand that's annoying" |
| **crash / crash again** | 技术 bug 描述 | "it's still trivially to crash" |
| **sluggish** | 性能批评 | "This feels sluggish" |

### 2.3 态度表达模式

- **坦率承认不足**：不回避自己的缺点和错误。
  - "Honestly, I'm failing quite hard."
  - "I allowed myself to be sucked into the vortex for a while."
  - "I basically never revert or use checkpointing."
  - "most code I don't read"
  - 来源: https://steipete.me/posts/just-one-more-prompt, https://steipete.me/posts/2025/shipping-at-inference-speed
  - 可信度: ★★★★★

- **先扬后抑再总结**：评价工具时的经典三段式。
  - "Gemini can be great, but its edit tools are too messy, so using it less and less."
  - "Claude often makes a mess but it's equally great in refactoring and cleaning up."
  - "codex isn't perfect — it sometimes overwrites lines of text — but it already nails the part that matters most"
  - 来源: https://steipete.me/posts/2025/optimal-ai-development-workflow, https://steipete.me/posts/2025/signature-flicker
  - 可信度: ★★★★★

## 三、幽默方式

### 3.1 自嘲式幽默

Peter 最突出的幽默风格是自嘲——把自己放在"上瘾者"的位置。

- **Claudoholic 梗**：将自己比作戒酒互助会成员。
  - "Hi, my name is Peter and I'm a Claudoholic. I'm addicted to agentic engineering. And sometimes I just vibe-code."
  - 来源: https://steipete.me/posts/just-one-more-prompt
  - 可信度: ★★★★★

- **slot machine 隐喻**：将 AI 编码比作赌博成瘾。
  - "the pendulum did swing heavily in the other direction"
  - "my new journey how to better control my slot machine addiction. Honestly, I'm failing quite hard."
  - "I literally built something so I have better access to my drug."
  - 来源: https://steipete.me/posts/just-one-more-prompt
  - 可信度: ★★★★★

- **Black Eye Club**：自嘲凌晨 4 点还在写代码的朋友圈。
  - "I call them the Black Eye Club. And on Reddit there's plenty of reports as well, about people that can't sleep anymore."
  - 来源: https://steipete.me/posts/just-one-more-prompt
  - 可信度: ★★★★★

### 3.2 冷幽默 / 讽刺

- **反讽 Lobsters 封禁**：
  - "Yes, I asked ChatGPT to make the header graphic extra AI-y, just because."
  - 来源: https://steipete.me/posts/2025/startup-slop
  - 可信度: ★★★★★

- **对工具的吐槽式幽默**：
  - "I tried Zed, just can't deal with a terminal that looks like this." (配图对比)
  - 来源: https://steipete.me/posts/2025/optimal-ai-development-workflow
  - 可信度: ★★★★★

- **夸张表达**：
  - "One week in AI feels like a month in the real world."
  - "I'm fun at parties when I tell people about the coming AGI apocalypse"
  - 来源: https://steipete.me/posts/just-one-more-prompt
  - 可信度: ★★★★★

### 3.3 Bug Report 中的幽默

Peter 在给 Apple 提 bug report 时也会加入幽默元素：
- "I often add a big button named 'Press me to crash' or 'Tap for tears.' Make it obvious."
- Dr. Who 主题的 bug report (rdar://25572553)
- "Bug reports don't need to be dry! Sometimes they are Dr. Who themed. Or have cat pictures."
- 来源: https://steipete.me/posts/2016/writing-good-bug-reports
- 可信度: ★★★★★

## 四、技术讨论中的表达方式

### 4.1 评价工具/框架的典型句式

Peter 评价技术工具时有一套固定的表达模式：

**模式A：承认优点 + 指出致命缺陷 + 给出结论**
- "Gemini can be great, but its edit tools are too messy, so using it less and less."
- "Warp's mission is to 'reinvent the command line with AI'. They've built beautiful GPU-accelerated panels and smart autocomplete... The fundamental difference comes down to trust and execution flow."
- "OpenCode did some great engineering... It's not without downsides"
- 来源: https://steipete.me/posts/2025/optimal-ai-development-workflow, https://steipete.me/posts/2025/signature-flicker
- 可信度: ★★★★★

**模式B：具体技术细节 + 个人体验 + 行动建议**
- "VS Code's terminal is too unstable, had plenty freezes when pasting in large amounts of text. Nothing beats Ghostty."
- "Codex can't search (asking 'google best practices' is usually better than context)."
- 来源: https://steipete.me/posts/2025/optimal-ai-development-workflow
- 可信度: ★★★★★

**模式C：竞品对比时的坦率**
- 在 TUI/终端渲染方案对比中，逐一分析 Amp、Gemini、OpenCode、Codex、pi 的优缺点，给出明确 verdict。
- 来源: https://steipete.me/posts/2025/signature-flicker
- 可信度: ★★★★★

### 4.2 技术深度表达

Peter 在技术文章中展现出深厚功底，但避免炫技：

- **用具体数据说话**：
  - "On my 2.4 GHz 8-Core Intel Core i9 MacBook Pro, it takes longer than a second to update the main view when changing the selection."
  - "Of the 10 seconds captured, 30 percent of them are used for the various retain/release and malloc calls in Swift and Objective-C."
  - 来源: https://steipete.me/posts/2020/state-of-swiftui
  - 可信度: ★★★★★

- **引用 crash log / 堆栈**：在 SwiftUI 分析中直接贴出完整堆栈，让读者自行判断。
  - 来源: https://steipete.me/posts/2020/state-of-swiftui
  - 可信度: ★★★★★

- **提出工作假设**：
  - "The general pattern here points to AppKit: The interaction between SwiftUI views and AppKit views seems to be poor."
  - 来源: https://steipete.me/posts/2020/state-of-swiftui
  - 可信度: ★★★★★

### 4.3 代码 Review / Bug Report 态度

- **强调复现性**：要求 bug report 必须有 "short, runnable sample"。
- **人性化表达**："Make it really obvious to trigger the issue"。
- **建设性而非指责**："Rants like 'nothing works' doesn't help anyone and just make it less likely that someone will work on your issue."
- **幽默中带专业**：Dr. Who 主题的 bug report 但核心信息清晰。
- 来源: https://steipete.me/posts/2016/writing-good-bug-reports
- 可信度: ★★★★★

## 六、对 AI/LLM 的态度

### 6.1 核心信念

- **AI 是范式转变**，不仅是工具升级：
  - "We're in the very early days of AI-native development tools. Claude Code represents a paradigm shift: from tools that help you run commands to tools that understand intent and take action."
  - "This isn't about AI replacing developers—it's about developers becoming orchestrators of incredibly powerful systems."
  - 来源: https://steipete.me/posts/2025/claude-code-is-my-computer
  - 可信度: ★★★★★

- **模型之间有真实差异，不迷信 benchmark**：
  - "IMO it's getting harder and harder to trust benchmarks - you need to try both to really understand."
  - 来源: https://steipete.me/posts/2025/shipping-at-inference-speed
  - 可信度: ★★★★★

- **迭代式构建，不追求完美架构**：
  - "Building software is like walking up a mountain. You don't go straight up, you circle around it and take turns, sometimes you get off path and have to walk a bit back, and it's imperfect, but eventually you get to where you need to be."
  - 来源: https://steipete.me/posts/2025/shipping-at-inference-speed
  - 可信度: ★★★★★

### 6.2 对特定模型的评价

## 八、表达 DNA 总结

### 8.1 一句话概括

**技术极客的坦率自白：用短句说真话，用自嘲化解严肃，用数据支撑观点，用故事传递信念。**

### 8.2 核心表达特征

| 维度 | 特征 | 强度 |
|------|------|------|
| **直接性** | 不废话，先给结论 | ★★★★★ |
| **坦率度** | 承认错误、暴露脆弱面 | ★★★★★ |
| **技术深度** | 数据+堆栈+具体工具名 | ★★★★★ |
| **自嘲幽默** | Claudoholic、slot machine、Black Eye Club | ★★★★★ |
| **口语化** | sth、bc、tho、ofc 等缩写 | ★★★★☆ |
| **节奏感** | 短句+破折号+并列结构 | ★★★★★ |
| **故事性** | 个人经历作为论据 | ★★★★☆ |
| **批判性** | 先扬后抑，建设性批评 | ★★★★★ |

### 8.3 模仿要点

1. **永远以 tl;dr 开头**——一句话总结全文
2. **短句为主**——每句不超过 20 词
3. **用具体数字和工具名**——不空谈
4. **自嘲>讽刺>自夸**——把自己放在"上瘾者"/"探索者"位置
5. **承认不确定性**——"I'm not sure", "we'll see", "I could be wrong"
6. **用隐喻解释抽象概念**——slot machine、walking up a mountain、drug
7. **评价工具用三段式**——优点+致命缺陷+行动建议
8. **个人故事>抽象论证**——用"我"的经历说明观点
9. **保持口语化但不低俗**——像在和朋友聊天，不是写论文
10. **emoji 极少使用**——偶尔用 🚀🦞🧿，但不泛滥
